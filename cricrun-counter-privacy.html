CricRun Counter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the CricRun Counter mobile application ("the App") developed by DevNest Tech.

1. Information we collect

The App does not directly collect personally identifiable information. Match data you create (scores, players, overs) is stored locally on your device unless you choose otherwise.

2. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob — to serve ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Google) — for analytics and crash reporting. See Firebase Privacy.

3. Permissions

The App may request network access to display ads and report crashes. No camera, contacts, or location data is accessed.

4. How data is used

Any data collected through third-party services is used to improve the App's performance, fix bugs, and serve relevant ads. We do not sell user data.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy questions about CricRun Counter, email devnestlabs16@gmail.com.
