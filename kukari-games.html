The games

Three classics, one app

9 Kukari (૯ કૂકરી)

Nine Men's Morris on the royal 24-point board. Form a trik (3 in a row) to capture opponent kukaris, then fly when only three are left.

4 Kukari (૪ કૂકરી)

Fast 3×3 leap-and-capture duel. Place four kukaris each, leave one spot empty, then jump over your opponent to win.

Athiyo / Asht Changa

Ancient 5×5 cowrie-shell race for 2–4 players. Roll a Changa or Athiyo, capture to unlock the inner circle and race home.
