Quick WP — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Quick WP ("Quick Whatsapp Message") mobile application ("the App") developed by DevNest Tech.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect, transmit or store any personal information on our servers. The App does not require an account, login, or contact/phone permission. Any content you create — phone numbers you enter, message templates, favorites and send history — is stored only in local storage on your own device and is never sent to DevNest Tech or any third party.

2. How the App sends messages

When you tap send, the App opens a chat using WhatsApp's official wa.me link with your message pre-filled, handing off to the WhatsApp app (or your browser if WhatsApp isn't installed). The App does not automate WhatsApp, read your WhatsApp messages, or send anything on your behalf without your final tap inside WhatsApp.

3. Third-party services

The App does not include any advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only third-party service involved is:

  • Google Play In-App Updates — used only to check for and prompt new versions of the App through the Play Store. This is handled entirely by Google Play services. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

The App does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location. It uses internet access only to open WhatsApp's wa.me link and to check for app updates via Google Play.

5. Data storage & deletion

All templates, favorites and history are stored locally on your device using on-device storage. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data from your device settings, permanently deletes this information. We have no copy of it.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children — consistent with Section 1, the App does not collect data from anyone.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page.

8. Contact
