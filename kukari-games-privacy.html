Kukari Games — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Kukari Games mobile application ("the App"), a collection of traditional Indian board games (9 Kukari, 4 Kukari and Athiyo / Asht Changa) developed by DevNest Tech.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect, transmit or store any personal information on our servers. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools.

2. Information stored on your device

To work properly the App stores the following only on your device, in the App's private storage:

  • Match history: the date, game type, result and the player names you type before a match.
  • Your settings: sound on/off, volume, sound style and vibration.
  • Small counters used to decide when to show a rating reminder or an update reminder.

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to DevNest Tech or any third party.

3. Third-party services

The App does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only third-party services involved are Google Play features, used when the App is installed from Google Play:

  • Google Play In-App Updates — checks for and prompts new versions of the App.
  • Google Play In-App Review — lets you rate the App on Google Play.

These are provided and processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information from them.

4. Permissions

The App requests a single permission: internet access, used only to check for updates via Google Play and to open links such as this policy or the Google Play page. Optional vibration feedback uses the standard system haptics and needs no permission; you can turn it off in Sound Settings.

5. Data storage & deletion

You can delete your match history at any time from the History screen. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data from your device settings, permanently deletes everything the App stored. We have no copy of it.

6. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing for us to share or delete.

7. Third-party links

The App can open links to external pages such as Google Play. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about Kukari Games, email devnestlabs16@gmail.com.
